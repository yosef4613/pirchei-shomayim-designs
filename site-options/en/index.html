About Pirchei Shomayim

Pirchei Shomayim is a boys’ choir and music program with groups in Jerusalem, Beit Shemesh and Beitar Illit. It gives boys who love to sing a place to develop their talent, build confidence and make music together.

What the boys learn
Voice technique, musical foundations and singing in harmony. The program also gives boys a chance to try writing, composing and recording in a studio.
From rehearsal to the stage
The boys record songs, make music videos and perform from time to time. Singing together teaches them to listen to one another and gives each boy a chance to be heard.
Music and Torah learning
The choir encourages musical growth alongside the boys’ studies in cheder. The songs are shared to bring joy and support listeners in their avodat Hashem.

Beitar Illit

Ata Bechartanu · Inside the filming

The Beitar Illit choir filmed its setting of the Rosh Hashanah Mussaf prayer at the Oderberg family home. Mordechai and Yisrael Meir Gordon wrote the melody.

Production notes and credits

Mordechai Gordon conducted and arranged the vocals. Avi Hess arranged the instrumental music.

Jerusalem

Petach Libi · At the Kotel and in the study hall

The Ramat Eshkol group spent the year working on the song and video. The filming includes prayers at the Western Wall and boys learning in pairs at Yeshivat Aish HaTorah in the Old City.

Production notes and credits

Mordechai Gordon composed and conducted the piece. Avi Deutsch arranged the music. The video is dedicated to Torah learners.

Makhalolam

At Makhalolam the audience joins in. The choir, Mordechai Gordon and Moishy Roth teach the audience their parts before everyone sings together.

Shema Kolinu is performed by the Jerusalem Pirchei Shomayim choir and the audience, with music by Rabbi Chaim Bennett and an arrangement by Moishy Roth.
